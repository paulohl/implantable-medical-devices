## Summary
The review describes remote monitoring of cardiac implantable electronic devices as an established clinical practice with growing alert and information volume. It surveys AI's role in loop-recorder and other device workflows, organized around monitoring burden, tools in clinical use, applications evaluated but not widely implemented, and future approaches including adaptive programming. The abstract highlights data quality, transparency, regulation and privacy as implementation barriers. It is a narrative review rather than an isolated trial showing that any named AI feature improves patient outcomes.
